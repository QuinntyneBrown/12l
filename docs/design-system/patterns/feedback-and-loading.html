Pattern

Feedback and loading

Every action gets an answer in the place the member is looking: inline for a field, on the button for a submit, a toast for something done elsewhere, a banner for something that lasts.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Feedback in 12L is quiet and close to the action. A member who taps Pay $94.00 sees that button work; a member whose price change saved sees a toast with Undo; a member who is offline sees a banner that stays until the connection comes back.

  • Inline (field errors, the reservation timer) for feedback about one thing on screen.
  • Button busy for the action just pressed; never a full-page spinner for a form submit.
  • alert inside the page for results of that page’s form: Draft saved, Profile saved, Your bank declined the card.
  • toast for something that finished and does not need the member now: Listing published · View listing.
  • Banner for a condition that persists across pages: offline, unverified email, payout returned.
  • dialog only when the member must decide before going on.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts stack at the bottom, full width with 16px margins on phones; from 640px they are 24rem wide at the bottom right.
  • Banners are full width under the top bar at every size; their actions wrap below the text on phones.
  • Skeletons use the same grid as the content (2-up listing cards on phones, 4-up on desktop).

Accessibility

  • Success and info messages use role="status" (polite); danger messages use role="alert". The toast region exists on page load so announcements are not lost.
  • Busy containers set aria-busy="true" and an aria-label (Loading listings); skeleton shapes are not announced.
  • Toasts pause their timer on hover and focus and are reachable by keyboard; error toasts never auto-dismiss (WCAG 2.2.1).
  • The reservation timer is aria-live="off" so it does not announce every second; threshold banners announce instead.
  • Reduced motion stops the skeleton shimmer and slide-ins; spinners keep turning because they carry meaning.

Content

  • Past tense for done things: Draft saved, Payment confirmed, Price updated to $40.
  • Name the object: Listing published · Air Force 1 ’07 is live.
  • Errors say what is safe: Couldn’t withdraw. Your listing is still live.
  • No Loading… text in skeletons; spinners carry a visually hidden label of what is loading.
