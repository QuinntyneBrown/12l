Pattern

Content and tone

12L talks like a friend who also wears a 12: short, plain, specific. It names the item, the amount and the date, and never makes the member work out what happens next.

Overview

This page applies the rules in content to whole screens. The mocks use one cast and one catalog (Marcus Bell, Dre Okafor, the Air Max 90, order 12L-48213) so every example reads as real.

  • Short: one idea per sentence; most UI strings are under ten words.
  • Specific: Pay $94.00, Ship by Fri 30 Oct, Reserved for you · 9:41 left.
  • Calm: no exclamation marks, no blame, no jokes in errors.
  • Fit first: sizes are always written out (Men’s US 12, Men’s L) because that is why members are here.

When to use what

SituationToneExample
Selling and buyingEncouraging, practicalList something you no longer wear, or find something that fits.
MoneyExact, neutral$33.30 after the 10% fee of $3.70
ErrorsCalm, what is safe, what to doCouldn’t withdraw. Your listing is still live.
Moderation and suspensionPlain, factual, no judgementYour account is suspended. You can still ship and track open orders. We emailed you the reason.
LegalPlain English, numberedMarketplace rules, Terms, Privacy

Responsive behaviour

  • Write for 360px first: button labels must not wrap there, titles may wrap to two lines.
  • Never shorten copy per breakpoint; if it does not fit on a phone it is too long everywhere.
  • Numbers and prices never break across lines (white-space: nowrap on .price).

Accessibility

  • Link text makes sense alone: View listing, Update bank details; never Click here or Learn more without an object.
  • Icon-only buttons have verb labels, with the object when several are on screen (Remove photo 2).
  • Abbreviations are expanded on first use or avoided; dates include the weekday.
  • Plain language (aim for grade 7) helps everyone, including members reading in a second language.

Content

  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons, titles and nav. Brand and model names keep their own casing (990v6, Arc’teryx).
  • Money: $85 in the catalog when there are no cents; $94.00 in checkout, orders and payouts.
  • Dates: Sun 25 Oct 2026, Fri 30 Oct within the year; times 10:42 ET.
  • Order and listing IDs in mono: 12L-48213, L-20931.
  • Use curly apostrophes and the en dash for ranges (3–30 letters).
