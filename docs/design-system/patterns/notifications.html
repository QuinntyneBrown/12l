Notifications

Toasts for things that just happened, banners for conditions that last, and the reservation timer for the one deadline 12L enforces.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • When to use what

Overview

12L has no notification centre; email carries anything a member must see later. On screen there are three channels: toasts on an ink surface with a coloured edge, full-width banners (the alert banner variant) under the top bar, and the cookie consent panel on first visit.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts: full width at the bottom with 16px margins on phones; 24rem at the bottom right from 640px.
  • Banners span the viewport; text and actions centre and wrap.
  • The consent panel is a bottom sheet on phones and a 26rem card at the bottom right from 768px.

Accessibility

  • The polite toast region is in the page from the start; danger toasts and banners use role="alert".
  • Toast actions and dismiss buttons are real buttons in the tab order; the focus ring flips to paper on the ink surface.
  • Auto-dismiss pauses on hover and focus and never applies to errors (WCAG 2.2.1). Toasts never hold the only path to an action: Add tracking also lives on the sale page.
  • The timer is role="timer" with aria-live="off"; threshold banners do the announcing.
  • Colour is never alone: each kind has its own icon and words.

Content

  • Toast title: the fact, past tense, under six words. Text: the object. Action: one verb.
  • Banner: one sentence with the consequence and the fix: Finish payout setup to receive $34.20. · Set up payouts.
  • Timer: Reserved for you · 9:41 left; at zero: Your reservation ended. You weren’t charged.
