Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

Overlays interrupt, so 12L uses them only for a decision that must happen before going on. Everything else is a page, a sheet or inline.

Overview

12L has three overlays: the modal dialog (a bottom sheet on phones), the full-screen menu and filter sheets (drawer), and the cookie consent panel. Tooltips and menus float but do not block. Every overlay sits on the z-order scale in elevation.

When to use what

JobUseExample
Confirm something irreversibleSmall dialog, danger icon, danger confirmWithdraw listing, Close account, Cancel order
Collect one to three fields tied to the current pageDialog with a formAdd tracking, Change password, Leave a review
Navigate on a phoneFull-screen menu sheetMenu
Refine a list on a phoneFilter sheet (left drawer from 768px)Filters
Anything long, shareable or with its own URLA pageCheckout, Sell an item, Report detail
Explain a controltooltipIcon-only buttons

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px dialogs are bottom sheets: full width, rounded top corners (--radius-xl), footer sticky at the bottom, buttons full width.
  • From 640px dialogs centre with 24px around them; sizes sm 24rem, md 32rem, lg 48rem; height caps at the viewport minus 48px and the body scrolls.
  • The filter sheet is full screen on phones and a 24rem left drawer from 768px; on 1024px+ filters are a sidebar and the sheet is not used.

Accessibility

  • role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby to the title, aria-describedby to the description when there is one.
  • Focus moves into the dialog, is trapped, and returns to the trigger on close. The page behind is inert.
  • Esc closes (except while an irreversible action is busy). The close button is labelled Close.
  • One dialog at a time. A dialog never opens another dialog; it replaces its content or hands over to a page.
  • Opening and closing use --duration-slow with the enter easing; reduced motion removes the slide.

Content

  • Titles are questions for confirmations (Withdraw this listing?) and verb phrases for tasks (Add tracking).
  • The confirm button repeats the verb of the title: Withdraw listing, never OK or Yes.
  • The safe button says what it keeps: Keep listing, Keep order.
  • Bodies state the consequence and what can be undone, in one or two sentences.
