Foundation

Responsive

Designed at 360px first and checked at 320px. Every screen works on a phone with one thumb, then gains columns as the viewport grows.

Tokens: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview
  • Component behaviour by breakpoint
  • Touch
  • Rules

Overview

Most 12L traffic is a phone in one hand. The base layout is a single column with 16px margins, a sticky top bar and, on listing detail, a sticky buy bar. Breakpoints add columns; they never remove content. See layout for the breakpoints and grid.

Touch

  • Interactive targets are at least 24×24px (2.5.8). Nav links, tabs, segmented options, accordion rows and star inputs are 44px.
  • Row actions in tables are always visible on devices without hover (@media (hover: none)).
  • Nothing depends on hover: tooltips also show on focus, and every hover-only reveal has a visible alternative.
  • Sticky bars stay under 20% of a 360×640 viewport.

Rules

  • Content reflows at 320px without horizontal scrolling (1.4.10); only tables and the gallery thumbnails scroll inside their own frame.
  • Never hide content at small sizes; move it (into the menu sheet, the filter sheet, below the fold).
  • Labels never truncate; listing titles and order meta may ellipsize because the full text is one tap away.
  • Pinch zoom is never disabled.
