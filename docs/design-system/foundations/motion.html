Foundation

Motion

Quick and quiet. Colour changes in 120ms, things appear in 200-320ms, nothing bounces, and everything but status indicators stops when the member asks for reduced motion.

Tokens: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easings
  • Choreography
  • Reduced motion
  • Tokens

Overview

Motion explains where something came from: a dialog rises from the bottom on phones, a drawer slides from its edge, a toast slides up into the corner. It is never decorative, and it never delays an action.

Durations

TokenValueUse
--duration-instantState flips with no transition
--duration-fastHover, colour, border, menus, tooltips
--duration-baseToasts, switches, backdrops, accordion
--duration-slowDialogs, sheets, drawers, photo zoom on card hover
--duration-deliberateProgress fills
--duration-cycle-fastOne spinner turn
--duration-cycleSkeleton shimmer, indeterminate progress, pulse

Easings

  • --ease-standard for things that move within the page.
  • --ease-enter (decelerate) for things that arrive: dialogs, toasts, sheets.
  • --ease-exit (accelerate) for things that leave: dismissed toasts.
  • --ease-spring is defined but unused in the mocks; keep it for a future celebratory moment, never for core UI.

Choreography

ElementEnterExit
Dialog (phone)Slide up 16px + fade, slow, enterInstant
Dialog (tablet+)Scale from 96% + fade, slow, enterInstant
Drawer / filter sheetSlide from its edge, slow, enterInstant
MenuScale from 96% at its origin, fast, enterInstant
ToastSlide up 16px + fade, base, enterFade + 8px down, base, exit
BackdropFade, base, enterInstant
Listing card hoverPhoto scales to 104%, slow, standardReverse

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the tokens set every transition duration to 0.01ms, so components need no extra media queries. components.css also caps animation durations, removes the skeleton shimmer and freezes indeterminate progress at full width. Two loops keep running because they are status, not decoration: the spinner and the loading button's spinner. Smooth scrolling is turned off.

Nothing flashes more than three times a second (2.3.1), and nothing moves for more than five seconds without a way to stop it (2.2.2): toasts pause their timer on hover and focus.
