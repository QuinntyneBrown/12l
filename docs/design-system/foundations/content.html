Content

12L talks like a friend who also wears a 12: short, plain, specific. Sentence case, real numbers, verbs on buttons, and no filler.

Overview

Copy is part of the design. The mocks use short real copy and nothing that fills space; this page writes down the rules they follow. For the full pattern see content and tone.

Voice and tone

We areSo we writeNot
PlainYour order shipped.Your order has been successfully dispatched!
SpecificShips by Fri 30 Oct.Ships soon.
Calm about problemsYour bank declined the payment. Try another card.Oops! Something went wrong :(
On the member's sideWe'll refund you within 5 business days.Refunds are subject to processing.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons, titles and tabs. Proper nouns keep their capitals (Red Wing, Patagonia).
  • Buttons are verbs: Buy now, List item, Add tracking, Confirm receipt.
  • Sizes are written US 12 for shoes and L for clothes; long form Men's US 12, Men's L.
  • Money: $85 in the catalog when there are no cents, $94.00 in checkout and totals, always with the currency symbol.
  • Dates: Sun 25 Oct 2026; drop the year in the current year when space is tight (Fri 30 Oct). Relative times only for recent things: 2 hours ago, Yesterday.
  • Times: 12-hour with lowercase am/pm, 3:40 pm. Countdowns use tabular figures: 9:42.
  • References are monospace and uppercase: 12L-48213, L-20931.
  • Counts: numerals always (3 reviews, 1 review), thousands separators from 1,000.

Error message formula

What happened + how to fix it, in one or two short sentences, next to the thing that failed.

SituationMessage
Empty required fieldEnter a title.
FormatEnter an email address like name@example.com.
Out of rangeEnter a price between $5 and $5,000.
Server failedWe couldn't save your changes. Try again.
Card declinedYour bank declined the payment. Try another card.

Never blame ("invalid input"), never use codes alone, never "Oops".

Empty-state formula

Title (what's here, or not) + one sentence (why, or what will appear) + one action.
