Foundation

Iconography

52 line icons on a 24-unit grid with a 2px square-ended stroke that inherits the text colour. Icons support a label; they rarely replace one.

Tokens: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview
  • The set
  • Sizes
  • Usage and labelling
  • Accessibility

Overview

Icons are inline SVG (<svg class="icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24">) with fill: none, stroke: currentColor, 2px stroke and round caps and joins. Rectangles in the set (lock, mail, card, calendar) are square-cornered, echoing the logo.

The set

Every icon the mocks use. The source of truth is ICONS in docs/mocks/_build/mk.py.

Sizes

.icon--sm 16px (chip remove, toast close) · .icon 20px default · .icon--lg 24px (empty states, dialog icons). Inside buttons icons are 1.25em.

Usage and labelling

  • Decorative icons next to text get aria-hidden="true"; the text is the label.
  • Icon-only controls (close, menu, search, more, remove photo) are buttons with an aria-label; the SVG stays hidden.
  • Meaningful standalone icons get role="img" and aria-label. The star rating is one image labelled "Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 23 reviews", not five icons.
  • Status icons pair with colour and words: info, check-circle, alert and x-circle in alerts and toasts.
  • Icons take the colour of their text. Don't colour an icon differently from its label except status icons in alerts and toasts.
  • New icons: draw on the 24 grid, 2px stroke, round caps, square rectangles; add to ICONS and to this sheet.

Accessibility

  • Icon-only targets are at least 24×24px (2.5.8) and 40px in the top bar.
  • Icons used as the only indicator of state (filled star) have a text equivalent in the accessible name.
  • Icons keep 3:1 against their background because they use the text colours.
