Foundation

Shape

Square like the logo. Corners are 0 to 4px, never soft, and one 45° chamfer — cut from the L — marks the size tag and the order confirmation.

Tokens: tokens/tokens.css
  • Overview

Overview

The logo is built from square blocks and one diagonal cut. The UI follows: photos and size tags are square, controls have a 2px radius that reads as square but softens anti-aliasing, cards and dialogs 4px, and the only big radius is the top of a phone bottom sheet.

--radius-sm and --radius-md share a value today; they stay separate tokens so controls and small chips can diverge later without renaming. Five distinct values in total.

The chamfer

--chamfer (10px) cuts the bottom-right corner at 45°, the same cut as the foot of the logo's L. It appears on the size tag and the confirmation mark only; anything more and it stops meaning 12L.

Usage

  • Never round a photo or a size tag.
  • Don't use --radius-full for buttons or chips: pills are not part of 12L.
  • Nested shapes keep the same or a smaller radius than their container.
