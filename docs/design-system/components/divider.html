Divider

A hairline that separates groups of content where white space alone is not enough: reviews in a dialog, order rows, form sections.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<hr>

Overview

12L prefers space to lines. Use a divider when two adjacent blocks would otherwise read as one: a buyer's and a seller's statement in the resolve-problem dialog, the total row of an order summary. Lists (orders, settings, reviews) draw their own row borders; do not add dividers between their items.

  1. Rule1px --color-border-default, 24px space above and below.

Sizes

One weight: --border-width-hairline. Spacing comes from the surrounding stack.

States

Dividers are static and have no interactive states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of their container at every breakpoint.
  • Vertical dividers become horizontal (or disappear) when the row wraps below 640px.

Theming

--color-border-default: paper-300 in light, ink-700 in dark. Decorative, so not held to a contrast minimum.

Role and pattern

<hr> (implicit role="separator") when it marks a thematic break. Purely decorative lines are borders on the containers instead. Vertical and labelled dividers use role="separator" explicitly.

Keyboard

Not focusable.

Focus

None.

Labelling

Labelled dividers carry the label as text; plain ones need none.
