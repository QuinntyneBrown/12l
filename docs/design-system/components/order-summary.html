Component · 12L brand

Order summary

What is being bought or paid out, and the arithmetic: an item row, then label–amount rows ending in a ruled total.

Overview

The order summary appears in the checkout's right column, on order and sale detail, and as the payout panel. It is built from two parts: an item row (thumb, title, brand and size) and a totals description list.

  • Buyers see Item, Shipping, Sales tax, Total.
  • Sellers see Item and shipping, 12L fee (10%) as a negative amount, Your payout.
  • Every amount has cents; the total row is bold with a rule above it.

Anatomy

  1. Title.panel__title: Summary or Payout; labels the <aside>.
  2. Item rowphoto thumb + title (one line, ellipsis) + brand, size and condition.
  3. Lines<div><dt><dd> pairs; labels muted, amounts tabular.
  4. Total.totals__total: rule above, bold, label in the default colour.

Responsive behaviour

  • On checkout below 1024px the summary stacks above the payment step so the total is seen before the card form; from 1024px it is the right column (2 of 5).
  • Labels and amounts stay on one line; long item titles truncate in the item row, never the amounts.
  • Panel padding grows from --space-6 to --space-8 at 768px.

Theming

Panels use --color-bg-surface (summary) or --color-bg-surface-sunken (payout); labels --color-fg-muted; the total and amounts --color-fg-default; the rule --color-border-default.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<aside aria-labelledby> containing a <dl>. Each row wraps one <dt>/<dd> pair in a <div> (valid HTML) so label and amount are read together.

Keyboard

Not interactive. The item title may be a link to the listing.

Focus

Not applicable.

Labelling

Row labels say what the amount is (Sales tax, 12L fee (10%)). The negative fee uses the Unicode minus so it is read as minus 3 dollars 70.

Content

  • Buyer rows: Item, Shipping, Sales tax, Total. Seller rows: Item and shipping, 12L fee (10%), Your payout.
  • The fee always states the rate in brackets and is 10% of item plus shipping, rounded to the cent.
  • Item meta: Nike · Men's US 12 · Used - Excellent.
  • Under a payout, say where and when it went: Sent Wed 7 Oct to Chase ending 6610.
