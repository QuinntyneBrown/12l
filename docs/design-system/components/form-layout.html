Form layout

How fields are arranged into a form: titled sections, two-up rows, the error summary at the top and an actions bar at the end that can stick to the bottom.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.form-section · .form-row · .form-actions

Overview

Forms are one column, capped at 44rem (.page--form) or 24rem on auth screens. Group long forms into .form-sections with an h2; pair short related fields in a .form-row. See the forms pattern for validation timing and submit behaviour.

  • Error summary (.form-summary): an alert at the top listing every error as a link to its field; receives focus on a failed submit.
  • Actions (.form-actions): end-aligned by default; --split puts a secondary action at the start (Save draft · List item); --start for settings pages; --sticky keeps them on screen in long forms.

Anatomy

  1. Section heading--text-h4; sections separated by a 1px rule and --space-10.
  2. FieldFull width; --space-6 between fields.
  3. Form rowTwo fields side by side from 640px.
  4. ActionsPrimary last; split, sticky or start-aligned variants.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column below 640px; .form-row splits into two columns from 640px.
  • Sticky actions (--sticky) sit at the bottom of the viewport on long forms, with a top rule and canvas background.
  • Below 640px, split actions keep their order; primary buttons may become block in auth forms (.btn--block).

Theming

Section rules and the sticky bar edge use --color-border-default; the sticky bar background is --color-bg-canvas so content scrolls under it. The summary uses alert danger tokens with a --border-width-heavy leading edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form novalidate> with custom messages; sections are <section aria-labelledby>; the summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1" and receives focus after a failed submit; its links target field ids.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFields in visual order, then actions.
EnterIn a text field, submits.
Summary link + EnterMoves focus to the field with the error.

Focus

Never move focus while the member types; only on submit (to the summary) or on success (to the result heading).

Labelling

The summary title says what to do: Fix these to continue; errors read the same in the summary and under the field.

Motion

Touch

Actions are 40–48px tall with 12px between.

Content

  • Section titles are nouns: Photos, Item, Price and shipping.
  • Primary action names the outcome: List item, Save profile, Join 12L.
  • The summary lists errors in field order.
