Chip

Chips show the filters applied to the catalog and let the member remove one at a time, or toggle a quick filter on and off.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablearia-pressed

Overview

Use chips for filter values a member can see and undo at a glance. Choosing one option from a set inside a form is a radio group or a button group; a read-only label is a badge.

  • Applied filter: ink chip with a labelled remove button; the row ends with Clear all. Catalog toolbar after filtering.
  • Filter toggle: a <button aria-pressed> that turns a quick filter on and off (Free shipping).

Anatomy

  1. LabelThe filter value as the member chose it, --text-label.
  2. Remove button20px round hit area inside the chip, labelled Remove filter New with tags.

Responsive behaviour

  • The chip row wraps; it never scrolls sideways, so every applied filter stays visible.
  • Below 1024px the filters live in the filter sheet, and the applied chips stay on the catalog toolbar above the grid.
  • Clear all appears once two or more filters are applied.

Theming

Component tokens --chip-bg, --chip-fg and --chip-border alias surface, default text and strong border; pressed chips switch to --color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent. In dark a pressed chip is paper with ink text, like the primary button.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Toggle chips are <button type="button" aria-pressed>. Applied-filter chips are a <span> holding a real remove <button>; the chip itself is not focusable.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to each toggle chip, or to each chip's remove button.
Space / EnterToggles the chip, or removes the filter.
After removalFocus moves to the next chip's remove button, or to Clear all, or to the toolbar heading when none remain; the result count is announced politely.

Focus

Standard focus ring on the chip (toggle) or on the remove button.

Labelling

Remove buttons say what they remove: Remove filter Shoes. Toggles are named by their label; the pressed state is announced by aria-pressed.

Touch

Default chips are 32px tall; small chips (24px) meet the minimum and are admin-only. The remove button's 20px icon area sits inside the 32px chip.

Content

  • Labels repeat the filter value exactly: New with tags, $20–$200 (en dash for ranges).
  • No category prefix unless two filters could share a value (Shoes, not Category: Shoes).
