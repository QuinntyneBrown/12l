Video poster

The video’s poster frame as one large button that opens the video dialog. Nothing plays until it is pressed.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

The video’s poster frame as one large button that opens the video dialog. Nothing plays until it is pressed. Used on the marketing page at /welcome.

Anatomy

  1. Button filling a 16:9 frame
  2. Poster image (decorative, alt="")
  3. Play label: icon and “Watch the 30-second pitch”
  4. Length tag “0:30”

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Default, hover (label lightens), focus (paper outline), active. The video never autoplays.

Responsive behaviour

Fills its column at 16:9.

Theming

Ink frame in both themes.

Accessibility

A native button named by its visible label; Enter and Space open the dialog through DialogService, which returns focus here on close.

Content

Say the length in the label.

Do and don’t

  • Do keep it on the marketing page.
  • Don’t reuse it for app screens, which use the page header and panels.

Tokens

--color-showcase-start, --color-fg-showcase, --color-fg-showcase-muted, --radius-lg, --shadow-4, --space-4, --control-height-lg.

Code

<tl-video-poster poster="/media/12l-pitch-poster.png" label="Watch the 30-second pitch" duration="0:30" (play)="open()" />
