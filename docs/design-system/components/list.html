List

Lists show one record per row: purchases and sales, listings, account settings, the moderation queue, the marketplace rules.

Overview

Lists are 12L's main way to show collections outside the catalog grid. Each row is read on its own, so they work at 360px where a table would not. The listing card grid is for browsing items; lists are for managing them.

  • Order list (.order-list): a whole-row link with an item row (thumbnail, title, meta) and an end column with price and status. Purchases, sales, my listings, payouts.
  • Settings list (.settings-list): a title, a current value and one action per row. Account settings, email preferences.
  • Queue (.queue): the admin moderation queue; an order-list row that wraps and carries a badge, a reporter count and the age.
  • Simple list (.list): title and meta with an optional trailing status; open reports on a member's admin page.
  • Rules (.rules-list): a numbered <ol> of title and explanation.

Anatomy

  1. Thumbnail.thumb: 56px square photo (80px with .thumb--lg), decorative alt="" because the title names the item.
  2. Title.item-row__title: --text-label at 16px, truncates with an ellipsis.
  3. Meta.item-row__meta: who and when, --text-body-sm muted, joined with a middle dot.
  4. AmountA price with cents, tabular figures.
  5. StatusA badge status: square dot plus word.

Responsive behaviour

  • Item rows never wrap the title; it truncates. The end column (price, status) stays on the right at every width.
  • Queue rows wrap at 640px: the item row takes the first line, badge, count and age the second.
  • Settings rows wrap their action under the text when the row is narrower than 16rem of text plus the button.
  • Lists are full width inside .page--form (44rem max) so long lines stay readable on desktop.

Theming

Lists read --color-border-default for dividers, --color-fg-muted for meta and --color-bg-subtle for hover on simple lists. They have no surface of their own: they sit on the canvas or inside a card. Photos keep their light studio backdrop in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<ul role="list"> (the role restores list semantics that Safari drops when list-style: none), or <ol> for ordered rules. Interactive rows are one <a> each; a row with its own action (settings) is not itself a link.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves from row link to row link, or to each row's action in a settings list.
EnterOpens the record.
Screen-reader list keysAnnounce the item count (list, 3 items) and move item by item.

Focus

The whole row link gets the standard focus ring with a 2px offset, so the thumbnail and status are inside it.

Labelling

The row link's name is its text: title, meta, price and status read in order. Thumbnails are alt="". Counts that are only numbers carry an aria-label (3 reporters).

Contrast

Title on canvas
Meta on canvas
Meta on hovered row
Shipped status on canvas

Motion

None. Hover changes the underline or background instantly.

Touch

Order and queue rows are at least 80px tall; settings actions are 40px buttons.

Content

  • Titles are the item's title as listed, never rewritten: Iron Ranger 8111 boots.
  • Meta answers who and when, joined with a middle dot: from vintage_lou · Thu 22 Oct.
  • Settings meta shows the current value, not a description: Changed Tue 2 Jun, Signed in on 2 devices.
  • Drafts without a title say so: Untitled draft · Tops; a missing price is an em dash.
