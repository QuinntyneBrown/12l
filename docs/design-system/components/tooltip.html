Tooltip

A short label that appears on hover and focus to name an icon-only button. Core; the mocks rely on aria-label alone today.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="tooltip"Core (not in mocks)

Overview

Icon-only buttons (Share, Save, Close, More actions) need a visible name for sighted pointer and keyboard users; a tooltip shows the same words as the button's aria-label. Tooltips never hold anything interactive or anything the member must read to finish a task; that is a popover or inline help.

  • Text: one to three words.
  • With shortcut: the label plus a <kbd> hint, for admin tools.

Anatomy

  1. BubbleInverse surface, caption text, 4px × 8px padding, 2px radius, 5px arrow.
  2. TriggerThe icon button it names.

Responsive behaviour

  • Touch devices have no hover: the tooltip appears on long press only, and never replaces a visible label where space allows.
  • Tooltips flip below the trigger when there is no room above.

Theming

--color-bg-inverse with --color-fg-inverse: ink on paper in light, paper on ink in dark, always the opposite of the page.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Tooltip: the bubble has role="tooltip" and the trigger references it with aria-describedby. The trigger keeps its own aria-label, so the name never depends on the tooltip.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the trigger shows the tooltip.
EscHides the tooltip without moving focus (1.4.13).

Focus

Focus stays on the trigger; the tooltip is not focusable.

Labelling

Tooltip text matches the aria-label; when they are identical, aria-describedby may be omitted to avoid double announcement.

Contrast

Tooltip text

Motion

Fades in over --duration-fast after a 300ms hover delay; hides immediately. Stays visible while the pointer is over it (1.4.13 hoverable).

Touch

Not relied on; triggers are 40px buttons.

Content

  • Same words as the button's label, sentence case, no full stop: Share listing.
  • Never instructions or policy text.
