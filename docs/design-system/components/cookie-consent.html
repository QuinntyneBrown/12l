Cookie consent

A small ink panel on a visitor's first visit: necessary cookies only until they choose, two equal choices and a link to the details.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNon-modalInverse surface

Overview

Consent is a non-modal region, bottom right (full width on phones), on the inverse surface so it is clearly separate from the page. The page stays usable; nothing waits for an answer. Accept analytics and Necessary only are equally easy. After a choice the panel closes and a toast confirms (Cookie choice saved). Details live in the cookie settings dialog, also linked from the footer.

Anatomy

  1. TitleCookies, h2 styled as h4, labels the region.
  2. TextOne sentence in --text-body-sm.
  3. ChoicesPrimary Accept analytics and secondary Necessary only, re-skinned for the inverse surface.
  4. Settings linkOpens the cookie settings dialog.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px the panel spans the viewport with 16px margins at the bottom; from 768px it is 26rem, 24px from the bottom-right corner.
  • Buttons wrap to two rows on narrow phones; both stay full height.

Theming

The panel reads --color-bg-inverse / --color-fg-inverse and re-points the button tokens: secondary buttons get a transparent fill and --color-border-inverse edge; the primary becomes paper on ink (ink on paper in dark). Focus rings use --color-fg-inverse.

.consent .btn          { --btn-bg: transparent; --btn-fg: var(--color-fg-inverse); --btn-border: var(--color-border-inverse); }
.consent .btn--primary { --btn-bg: var(--color-fg-inverse); --btn-fg: var(--color-bg-inverse); --btn-border: var(--color-fg-inverse); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> landmark, not a dialog: it does not trap focus or block the page. It is placed at the end of <body>, after the skip link targets, so keyboard users reach the page first and the region via landmarks.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Accept analytics, Necessary only and Cookie settings.
Enter, SpaceMakes the choice; the panel closes and focus returns to where it was before (or the page heading).

Focus

Focus rings switch to --color-fg-inverse on the ink panel so they remain 3:1+.

Labelling

Region named by its Cookies heading. Both choices name the outcome.

Content

  • Choices are equal and plain: Accept analytics, Necessary only. No Accept all dark pattern.
