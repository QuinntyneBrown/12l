Radio group

One answer from a short list where every option should be read: a cancellation reason, a report decision, the shipping address at checkout.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablefieldset + native radios

Overview

Use radios for two to seven options with exactly one answer. With short labels that fit side by side use the segmented button group; for long known lists use a select.

  • List (.choice): reasons and decisions in dialogs.
  • With description (.choice__text): admin decisions that need a consequence line.
  • Option cards (.option): the whole card is the target; used for addresses at checkout where the option contains rich content.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question.
  2. Radio20px circle; checked fills ink with a paper dot.
  3. Option cardSurface, 1px edge; checked edge doubles in --color-fg-default.

Sizes

One size: 20px controls, rows at least 24px; option cards pad with --space-4.

States

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Lists stay vertical at every width.
  • Option cards fill the column; they stack, never sit side by side, so long addresses wrap cleanly.

Theming

Radios share checkbox tokens. Option cards read --color-bg-surface and --color-border-default, hover --color-border-strong, and the checked card draws an inset 1px ring in --color-fg-default on top of its edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios sharing a name inside <fieldset>/<legend> (APG Radio Group behaviour from the browser). Option cards are <label> elements wrapping the radio, so the whole card toggles it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the group (the checked radio, or the first), then out.
↑ ↓ / ← →Moves and checks.
SpaceChecks the focused radio.

Focus

Ring around the radio circle; option cards also show it on the radio, which sits at the card's start.

Labelling

Legend names the group; error linked from the fieldset with aria-describedby.

Contrast

Radio edge on page
Checked fill
Card text

Motion

Edge and fill transition over --duration-fast.

Touch

Option cards are large targets; list rows are at least 24px with 8px gaps.

Content

  • Options are parallel phrases of similar length.
  • Order by likelihood, or logically; never preselect a consequential choice such as a cancellation reason.
  • Describe consequences in the description line, not the label.
