Component · 12L brand

Review

A buyer's rating and words about a seller, with at most one public reply. The same quote and thread parts carry problem reports and cancellation requests.

Overview

Reviews build trust in a peer marketplace, so they are plain and attributable: who, how many stars, when, what they said. Sellers can reply once, publicly. Two smaller parts share the look:

  • Quote (.quote): one member's words shown inside a dialog or order page, with <cite> naming them (a cancellation reason, a problem description).
  • Thread (.thread): a short exchange between buyer and seller on a problem, oldest first, in sunken blocks.

Anatomy

  1. Headavatar (sm), author link, stars and value, date.
  2. Ratingrating for this one review, no count.
  3. TextBody text, the buyer's words unedited.
  4. Reply.review__reply: 2px rule on the left, muted small text, prefixed Reply:.

Responsive behaviour

  • The head wraps: stars and date drop under the name on narrow screens (flex-wrap with --space-3 gaps).
  • Reviews are a single column at every width, capped at the reading measure on profile pages.

Theming

No surface: reviews sit on the canvas separated by --color-border-default rules. Replies and quotes use a left rule (--color-border-default 2px for replies, --color-border-strong 3px for quotes); thread items use --color-bg-surface-sunken.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each review is an <article> in a list; the date is a <time>. Quotes are <blockquote> with <cite>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the author link, then the Reply link when present.

Shared ring on the links.

Labelling

The stars are role="img": Rated 5 out of 5. The reply starts with a bold Reply: so it is distinguishable without the rule.

Content

  • Show the buyer's words as written; 12L moderates abuse through reports, not edits.
  • Dates as Mon 5 Oct; add the year only when it is not the current one.
  • Quotes use typographic quotes (“ ”) and cite the member handle, not the real name.
  • Thread captions: You · Sat 24 Oct for the viewer, the handle for the other member.
