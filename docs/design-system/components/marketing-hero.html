Marketing hero

The ink showcase band that opens and closes the marketing page: eyebrow, a 3 to 6 word headline, one supporting line, two calls to action and optional media.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

The ink showcase band that opens and closes the marketing page: eyebrow, a 3 to 6 word headline, one supporting line, two calls to action and optional media. Used on the marketing page at /welcome.

Anatomy

  1. Showcase surface: --gradient-showcase, ink in both themes
  2. Decorative tl-shapes behind the content
  3. Eyebrow in overline type
  4. Headline (h1 in the hero, h2 in the closing band) with an em accent
  5. Lede: one line
  6. Actions: primary and secondary buttons
  7. Media slot (the video poster) in the second column from 992px

Variants

Variants hero (two columns with media, h1) and closing (one column, h2, quiet shapes).

Men’s US 12 shoes · Men’s L clothing

Built for size 12 and L.

Big sizes, finally in one place.

Shop the catalogStart selling

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Static. Buttons keep their hover, active and focus states, re-pointed to the showcase tokens: a primary button is paper on ink and the focus ring is paper.

Responsive behaviour

One column below 992px with the media after the actions; two equal columns from 992px. Padding --space-16/--space-20, --space-24 from 992px.

Theming

The showcase stays ink in light and dark, like the logo, so its contrast never changes: paper text 17.4:1 on the dark end and 12.0:1 on the light end; accent 7.0:1 and 4.9:1 (display size only).

Accessibility

A section labelled by its heading. The headline is the page h1 (hero) or an h2 (closing). Shapes are aria-hidden. Contrast pairs are in tokens/contrast-pairs.json.

Content

Headline 3 to 6 words, one supporting sentence, two actions at most. Never promise sizes the catalog rejects: “built for size 12 and L”, not “only”.

Do and don’t

  • Do keep it on the marketing page.
  • Don’t reuse it for app screens, which use the page header and panels.

Tokens

--gradient-showcase, --color-fg-showcase, --color-fg-showcase-muted, --color-fg-showcase-accent, --space-16, --space-20, --space-24, --text-display, --text-overline, --text-body-lg.

Code

<tl-marketing-hero eyebrow="…" lede="Big sizes, finally in one place.">
  <ng-container title>Built for size <em>12 and L.</em></ng-container>
  <ng-container actions>…</ng-container>
  <tl-video-poster media (watch)="open()" />
</tl-marketing-hero>
