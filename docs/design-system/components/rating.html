Rating

Seller reputation as five stars, the average and the review count; and the star input buyers use to leave a review.

Overview

Two parts that share the star. Rating summarises a member's reviews wherever they are named as a seller (listing detail, profile, reviews). Star input collects one rating from 1 to 5 in the Leave a review dialog.

  • Stars are ink, not gold: the system's only accent is the red L, and ink stars match the type.
  • Zero reviews is a real state: No reviews yet in muted text, never five empty stars.
  • A seller with no completed sales shows a New seller badge instead of a rating.

Anatomy

  1. StarsFive 14px stars, filled to the rounded average; off stars use --color-border-strong.
  2. AverageOne decimal, bold.
  3. CountMuted, in brackets, with the noun: (128 reviews).

Responsive behaviour

  • The summary never wraps (white-space: nowrap); in a narrow seller block it moves to its own line under the city.
  • Star-input targets stay 44px at every width; on a 320px screen five stars plus the word fit in one row.

Theming

Filled stars use --color-fg-default (ink, or paper in dark); off stars use --color-border-strong. The star input's selected fill reads --color-fg-default too, so it inverts with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Display: role="img" with aria-label="Rated 4.9 out of 5 from 128 reviews"; the stars inside are aria-hidden. Input: native radio group in a <fieldset> with <legend>Rating</legend>; each visually hidden label says 4 stars, Great.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEnters the star group (the checked star, or the first).
← →Moves the selection one star.
SpaceSelects the focused star.

Focus

The focused star's icon gets the shared ring (the input itself is visually hidden).

Labelling

Each star's label names its number and word. The error Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars is linked with aria-describedby on the fieldset.

Content

  • 4.9 (128 reviews); 5.0 (1 review); No reviews yet. One decimal always.
  • Star words, low to high: Poor, Fair, Good, Great, Excellent.
  • Never round a 4.96 to 5.0 stars of fill and 4.9 in text; both use the same rounding.
