Price

Money, set in bold tabular figures. Whole dollars in the catalog, cents everywhere a member pays or gets paid.

Overview

.price styles an amount; the formatting rules below decide the string. Prices are always US dollars (12L ships within the US only).

  • Catalog (cards, search): whole dollars when there are no cents, $85.
  • Listing detail: $85.00 large, then a muted line $9.00 shipping · $94.00 total before tax.
  • Checkout, orders, payouts: always cents, in an order summary. Fees are shown as negative amounts with a true minus: −$3.70.

Anatomy

  1. Amount.price--lg: --text-h2, tight tracking, tabular figures.
  2. Shipping lineSmall muted text; the total uses .num so digits align.

Responsive behaviour

  • The large price does not shrink on phones; it is the second thing a buyer reads after the title.
  • Below 1024px the total (item + shipping) repeats in the sticky buy bar; it hides when the bar holds two buttons.
  • Prices never wrap: white-space: nowrap. A row that cannot fit truncates the title first.

Theming

Weight and figures, not colour, make a price: --font-weight-bold, font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, --color-fg-default. Muted amounts use --color-fg-muted and regular weight. Never colour a price green or red; payouts and refunds say what they are in words.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text in a <span>. Screen readers read $85.00 correctly; do not split the amount across elements.

Keyboard

Not focusable.

Focus

Not applicable.

Labelling

The words around the price carry its meaning: shipping, total before tax, Your payout. A minus fee uses the Unicode minus (−), which screen readers read as minus.

Content

  • $1,240 with a thousands separator; $85 not $85.00 in the catalog; $85.00 on detail and everywhere money moves.
  • Free shipping in words, never $0.00 shipping; in a totals row write Free.
  • total before tax on detail; tax appears only at checkout once the address is known.
  • Fees: 12L fee (10%) then −$3.70; payouts: Your payout $33.30.
