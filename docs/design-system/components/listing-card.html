Component · 12L brand

Listing card

One item for sale in the catalog grid: photo with size tag, brand, title, condition and price. The whole card is one link to the listing.

Overview

The listing card is the catalog's unit: profile pages, More in size 12 and search results all reuse it. It deliberately has no border, shadow or buttons: the photo carries the card, and the text below it answers the four questions a buyer scans for (brand, what it is, condition, price).

  • The whole card is a single <a>; nothing inside is separately clickable (no save heart, no seller link), so there is one tab stop per item.
  • Status is shown by the photo stamp (photo); price stays visible on reserved and sold items.
  • For lists of a member's own items (my listings, orders) use an item row instead; cards are for browsing.

Anatomy

  1. Photophoto 4:5 with the size tag; zooms to 104% on hover.
  2. Brand.listing-card__brand, overline, muted.
  3. Title.listing-card__title, body, one line with ellipsis.
  4. Condition.listing-card__condition, small, muted, truncates first.
  5. Priceprice, bold tabular, never truncates.

  • Title and condition truncate with an ellipsis; brand and price never do. Long titles are still the link's full accessible name.
  • On touch devices there is no hover zoom; nothing is hidden behind hover.

Theming

The card has no surface of its own: text uses --color-fg-default and --color-fg-muted on the canvas, the photo keeps its light backdrop in both themes. Change the grid density with .listing-grid gap tokens, not by adding borders to cards.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One <a href> wrapping the photo and text, inside <li> in a <ul class="listing-grid" role="list"> (the role restores list semantics that list-style: none removes in Safari).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves card to card in reading order (left to right, row by row).
EnterOpens the listing.

Focus

The ring is offset by --space-1 so it clears the photo edge and wraps photo and text together.

Labelling

The link's name is its text in order: Size US 12, Nike, Air Max 90, white, Used - Excellent, $85. The photo's alt adds brand and title; keep it, because the card may be the only place the image is described. Stamps are read as text (Reserved).

Contrast

Title on canvas
Brand and condition on canvas
Focus ring on canvas

Motion

Photo zoom over --duration-slow; removed under reduced motion. Skeleton shimmer stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Each card is far larger than 44px; the 16px column gap on phones prevents mis-taps.

Content

  • Brand as the seller entered it (New Balance, Arc'teryx).
  • Title without the brand or the size: 990v6, grey, not New Balance 990v6 size 12.
  • Condition from the fixed list: New with tags, New without tags, Used - Excellent, Used - Good, Used - Fair.
  • Price in whole dollars when there are no cents ($85); shipping is shown on the listing, not the card.
