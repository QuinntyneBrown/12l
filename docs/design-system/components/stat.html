Stat

A labelled number that summarises something: payouts paid and pending, a seller's sales and rating, what the seller earns after the fee.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableTabular figures

Overview

Use a stat when one number answers a question the member came with (How much is pending?). Keep it to two to four per row; more is a table.

  • Stats row (.stats): wrapping row of stats; values in --text-h3. Profile, member admin, payouts.
  • Stat on its own: value in --text-h2, optional delta (.stat__delta--up|--down) with arrow and sign.
  • Earnings (.earnings): one line under the price field on the sell form: You earn after the 10% fee — $61.20.

Anatomy

  1. Label--text-label, muted, above the value.
  2. Value--text-h2 (or --text-h3 in a row), tabular figures.
  3. Delta (optional)Arrow, sign and amount in success or danger text; never colour alone.

Responsive behaviour

  • Stats rows wrap with a 32px gap; at 360px a four-stat row becomes two rows of two.
  • Values never truncate; shorten the label instead.

Theming

Values read --color-fg-default, labels --color-fg-muted, deltas --color-success-fg and --color-danger-fg. Values use the display family through --text-h2/h3, the heavy numerals of the logo.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text: a label span and a value span inside a <div>. The label comes first in the DOM so it is read first. When a stats row is the main summary of a page, wrap it in a <dl>-like order (label then value) rather than value then label.

Keyboard

Not interactive.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The label is adjacent and read first. Deltas include the sign and a unit in words or symbols (+3 vs September), and the arrow is text, not an icon only.

Contrast

Motion

Values change without animation; no counting-up effects.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Labels are short nouns in sentence case: Paid out, Pending, Member since.
  • Money with cents and a dollar sign: $40.50. Counts as digits: 46.
  • Say what a derived number is derived from: You earn after the 10% fee.
