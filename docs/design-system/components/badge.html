Component · Data display

Badge

Badges and statuses label a record's state in a word: New seller, Hidden, Shipped, Sold. They are never buttons.

Overview

12L uses two forms. Pick by where the label sits:

  • Status (.status): a square dot and a word in the status colour, no background. Order and listing lifecycle in lists and headers: Active, Reserved, Sold, Shipped, Refunded.
  • Badge (.badge): an uppercase tag on a tinted block, for attributes and moderation flags: New seller, Hidden, Payout held.
  • Solid and count badges carry a filled background for counts and admin markers; pill count is the neutral square count in the moderation queue.

Neither is interactive. A filter you can toggle is a chip; a message is an alert.

Anatomy

  1. Dot6px round on badges, 8px square on statuses; currentColor.
  2. Badge label--text-overline, uppercase, wide tracking, on the tone's -bg.
  3. Status label--text-body-sm in the tone's -fg, sentence case.

Sizes

Badges and statuses have one size each; they take the caption and body-sm sizes of the rows they sit in.

States

Badges are static: no hover, focus or disabled state. Their tone is their state. Both forms in both themes:

Responsive behaviour

  • Badges never wrap (white-space: nowrap); keep labels to two words.
  • In order lists the status sits under the price in the end column at every width; in the queue it wraps to the second line with the count.

Theming

Each tone reads its status set: --color-{tone}-bg and --color-{tone}-fg for badges, --color-{tone}-fg for statuses. In dark the backgrounds become deep tints (--palette-green-950) and the text lightens (--palette-green-300), so the pairs stay above 7:1.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text in a <span>. No role: the word is the information, and the dot is decoration. A count badge gets an aria-label.

Keyboard

Not focusable.

Focus

Not focusable; a badge inside a link is part of that link's name.

Labelling

The label text is read as part of its row (Iron Ranger 8111 boots … $244.97 Shipped). Count-only badges say what they count: 3 reporters.

Content

  • One or two words. Statuses are past participles or adjectives in sentence case: Shipped, Reserved. Badges are nouns or adjectives: New seller, Hidden.
  • Use the same word for the same state everywhere: an order is Completed in purchases, sales and admin.
  • Never put an action in a badge (Click to verify).
