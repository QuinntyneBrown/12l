Component · 12L brand

Size tag

The ink block that says US 12 or L. Every 12L listing is men's US 12 (shoes) or men's L (everything else); the tag carries that promise with the logo's 45° cut.

Overview

The size tag is a label, not a control. It appears on every listing photo (bottom left), next to the condition on listing detail, and on category tiles. It is the same ink-on-paper block in both themes because it usually sits on a light studio photo.

  • Shoes read US 12; tops, outerwear and bottoms read L.
  • The number or letter is black-weight; the prefix (US) is the overline style.
  • There is no other size: the tag never shows 11.5, XL or a range. A listing in another size cannot exist on 12L.

Anatomy

  1. Prefix--text-overline, uppercase, wide tracking. Shoes only.
  2. Size<b>, black weight, one step larger than the prefix.
  3. Chamfer45° cut at the bottom right, --chamfer × 0.7 (default) by clip-path; padding on the right makes room for it.

Responsive behaviour

  • The tag never scales with the viewport; on a 2-up phone grid it is the same 12px tag as on desktop.
  • On photos it stays pinned to the bottom-left corner, flush with the edges, so it survives any crop.
  • On thumbnails smaller than 4rem (order rows, uploader tiles) the tag is omitted: the row text already says Men's US 12.

Theming

The tag reads --color-size-tag-bg and --color-size-tag-fg, fixed to ink and paper in both themes. Product photos keep a light backdrop in dark mode, so an ink tag stays readable on them; inverting it would put a paper block on a paper photo.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <span> with aria-label="Size US 12" (or Size L) so screen readers hear a word, not a fragment. Inside a listing card link, the label becomes part of the link text.

Keyboard

Not focusable; it is read as part of its card or row.

Focus

Not applicable to the tag; the card around it shows the ring.

Labelling

aria-label="Size US 12". Never rely on the shape or colour to say it is a size.

Content

  • Shoes: US 12 (prefix, space, number). Everything else: L.
  • In running text write Men's US 12 and Men's L; the tag is the short form.
  • Never pluralise, never add size inside the tag.
