Component · 12L brand

Buy bar

The listing's purchase actions. On phones it sticks to the bottom of the screen with the total; from 1024px it sits in the info column.

Overview

The buy bar keeps the one primary action of the listing page in reach while a buyer scrolls photos, description and seller details. It holds the total including shipping and at most two buttons.

  • Buyer: Buy now (primary, large) with $94.00 incl. shipping.
  • Guest: Sign in to buy.
  • Own listing: Edit listing and Withdraw; with two buttons the total is hidden to give them room.
  • Reserved or sold: no bar; the stamp and a status line replace it.

Anatomy

  1. Total.buy-bar__price: item plus shipping, bold tabular.
  2. Captionincl. shipping, caption style.
  3. ActionsLarge buttons that share the remaining width (flex: 1).

Responsive behaviour

ViewportBehaviour
< 1024pxposition: sticky; bottom: 0, full bleed (negative --layout-margin), surface background with a top rule, total shown when there is one button.
≥ 1024pxStatic inside the info column after the price; no background, rule or total.
  • The page reserves no extra bottom padding: sticky (not fixed) positioning releases the bar at the end of the info column.
  • Buttons share the width equally and never wrap; labels are short enough to fit at 320px (Buy now, Edit listing, Withdraw).

Theming

Background --color-bg-surface, top rule --color-border-default, layer --z-sticky. In dark mode the bar is ink-900 over the ink-950 canvas, so it reads as raised without a shadow.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <div> in the info column's DOM order, after the price, so keyboard and screen-reader order match the desktop layout even when it is visually pinned on phones. No landmark role.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the bar's buttons after the price, before the description.
Enter / SpaceActivates the button.

Focus

Shared ring on the buttons. When focus moves to an element under the pinned bar, the page scrolls it into view above the bar (scroll-padding-bottom equal to the bar height).

Labelling

Button labels stand alone (Buy now); the total's caption incl. shipping is plain text next to it.

Contrast

Total on bar
Caption on bar
Primary label

Motion

None; the bar does not slide in or hide on scroll.

Touch

48px buttons; the bar adds --space-3 padding so its full height is 72px.

Content

  • One primary verb: Buy now. Not Purchase, not Add to bag (12L has no cart).
  • Total caption is always incl. shipping; tax is added at checkout.
  • Own listing actions: Edit listing, Withdraw (danger, opens a confirmation).
