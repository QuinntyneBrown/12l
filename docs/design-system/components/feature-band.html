Component · Marketing

Feature band

A generous page band with an eyebrow, a short headline, an optional action and either up to three points or a projected body such as steps.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A generous page band with an eyebrow, a short headline, an optional action and either up to three points or a projected body such as steps. Used on the marketing page at /welcome.

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow with a small chamfered red marker
  2. Headline h2, 3 to 6 words
  3. Optional actions
  4. Points (bold title and one muted line each) or a projected body

Variants

default on the canvas, surface on white (alternating bands), stacked (body below the heading, for steps).

For shoppers

Everything already fits you.

  • Every pair is a men’s US 12Sneakers, boots and dress shoes.
  • Honest conditionFlaws named up front.

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Heading and body stack below 992px; 5:7 columns from 992px. Padding --space-16, --space-24 from 768px.

Theming

Semantic page tokens; works unchanged in light and dark.

Accessibility

A section labelled by its h2; points are a list; the marker is a pseudo-element with no content.

Content

Three points at most, each a few words plus one short line.

Do and don’t

  • Do keep it on the marketing page.
  • Don’t reuse it for app screens, which use the page header and panels.

Tokens

--space-16, --space-24, --text-h1, --text-overline, --text-h4, --color-brand, --color-border-default, --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-surface.

Code

<tl-feature-band eyebrow="For shoppers" heading="Everything already fits you." [points]="points">
  <a tlButton="primary" routerLink="/" actions>Browse the catalog</a>
</tl-feature-band>
