Switch

An on/off control for a setting: which emails 12L sends, whether analytics cookies are allowed. The label names the setting, not the action.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="switch" on a checkbox

Overview

Use a switch for a setting that is either on or off. In 12L switches sit in settings lists saved by a button (Save on Emails) or apply at once (cookie settings). For agreeing to something, use a checkbox.

  • Square track and thumb, echoing the logo; the checked track is ink (paper in dark).
  • Mandatory settings are shown on and disabled with the reason in the description (Always on).

Anatomy

  1. Label and description.choice__text: setting name and what it controls.
  2. Track and thumb44 × 24px track, 20px thumb; thumb slides 20px when on.

Sizes

One size: a 44 × 24px track in a row at least 24px tall.

States

ControlOffOnHoverFocusDisabled offDisabled on
Switch

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Rows keep label left, switch right at every width; the label wraps, the switch never moves below it.
  • In settings lists the switch aligns to the row's end.

Theming

Off track --color-border-strong, on track --color-accent, thumb --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1. Dark theme: paper track when on, ink thumb.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native checkbox with role="switch", so it announces on/off; inside a <label>. In the Emails list the visible row title names the switch through visually hidden text in the label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the switch.
SpaceToggles.

Focus

Ring around the track (2px, 2px offset).

Labelling

Name the setting (Analytics), never Turn on analytics; state is announced by the role.

Contrast

Off track on surface
On track on surface
Thumb on on-track

Motion

Thumb slides and track colour changes over --duration-base (200ms); reduced motion makes it instant.

Touch

The whole label row is the target.

Content

  • Labels are nouns for the setting: Reviews, News from 12L.
  • Descriptions say what is sent or tracked: When someone reviews you.
  • Explain disabled switches: Always on.
