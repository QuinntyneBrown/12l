Progress bar

A thin bar that shows how far a measurable task has got: a photo upload, a data export.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="progressbar"

Overview

Use a progress bar when the task has a known size and takes longer than about a second: uploading listing photos, preparing a data export. When the duration is unknown and short use a spinner; when content is loading into a known layout use a skeleton. Order progress (Paid → Shipped → Delivered → Completed) is a stepper, not a progress bar.

The mocks show no progress bar yet; it is in the system because photo uploads on the sell form (.uploader__progress) and the data export need one.

Anatomy

  1. LabelWhat is happening, above the bar; also the bar's aria-label.
  2. Value (optional)Percentage in tabular numerals.
  3. Track8px, --color-bg-subtle-hover, fully rounded.
  4. Fill--color-accent; width from --value.

Variants

Determinate, indeterminate, success and danger.
HTML
<div class="stack" style="width: min(100%, 24rem);"><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Uploading photos" aria-valuenow="35" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 35%;"></div>
</div>
<div class="progress progress--indeterminate" role="progressbar" aria-label="Preparing your data export" aria-busy="true"><div class="progress__bar"></div>
</div>
<div class="progress progress--success" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload complete" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 100%;"></div>
</div>
<div class="progress progress--danger" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload failed" aria-valuenow="70" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 70%;"></div>
</div>
</div>

Sizes

One height (8px). Inside uploader tiles the bar is inset 8px from the tile edges.

States

Variant0%50%CompleteFailedIndeterminate
Bar

Both themes

  • On failure the bar keeps its position in danger and an inline error names the file and offers Try again.
  • On success the bar disappears after a short pause; the thing it was making (the photo tile) is the confirmation.

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar is fluid: it fills its container at every breakpoint.
  • In the uploader grid (3 tiles across on phones, 4 from 640px) each tile has its own bar.

Theming

The track reads --color-bg-subtle-hover; the fill --color-accent, or --color-success-solid / --color-danger-solid. In dark the accent is paper on an ink track.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin="0", aria-valuemax="100" and an aria-label (or aria-labelledby pointing at the visible label). Indeterminate bars omit aria-valuenow and set aria-busy="true" on the region being updated.

Keyboard

Not focusable. A Cancel button, when offered, sits next to the bar.

Focus

None.

Labelling

The label says what is in progress and is announced on completion through a separate role="status" message (4 photos uploaded); the bar itself is not polled by screen readers.

Contrast

Fill on track
Value text

Motion

Width changes ease over --duration-slow. The indeterminate sweep runs on --duration-cycle and becomes a static 60%-opacity full bar under reduced motion.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • Label with a count: Uploading photo 2 of 4, Preparing your data export.
  • Show a percentage only when it is meaningful; never fake progress.
