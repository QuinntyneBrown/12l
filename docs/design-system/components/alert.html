Alert

Alerts put a lasting message where it applies: above a form after it failed, at the top of an order, or across the page as a banner when the whole site is affected.

Overview

Alerts stay on screen until the condition changes. Use an inline .alert for a message about one form, order or section; use .alert.banner above the top bar for something that affects the whole account or site (offline, maintenance, unverified email, payout problems). For a passing confirmation use a toast; for one field use an inline message; for a choice use a dialog.

  • Info: a fact about the state of things (Refunded in full, You’ve already reported this listing).
  • Success: the thing the member did is done and the page stays (Profile saved, Draft saved, Link sent).
  • Warning: something limits what they can do, with a way out (Verify your email to buy and sell, In someone’s checkout, Too many attempts).
  • Danger: something failed or is blocked (Your bank declined the card, Your account is suspended, Couldn’t withdraw).
  • Flaws note (.flaws): the warning-toned rule on a Used - Fair listing that states its flaws. It is content, not a status, so it has no icon or role.

Anatomy

  1. Leading edge4px rule (--border-width-heavy) in the tone border colour; 1px on the other sides.
  2. Icon20px, --alert-icon; can be swapped for a more specific one (mail, clock, lock, flag, wifi-off).
  3. Title--text-label; the whole message when it fits in one line.
  4. TextDetail and the way forward; may include a reference (.ref).
  5. Actions (optional)Small secondary button or underlined links in the tone colour.
  6. Dismiss (optional)Only for messages the member can safely forget.

Sizes

Alerts have one size; they take the width of their container. Banners always span the viewport.

States

Alerts are static; their states are about structure and interaction. The dismiss button's hover and focus are forced with data-state.

  • Appearing: an alert that results from a submit is inserted above the form and focused (tabindex="-1") when it is an error summary; otherwise it is announced by its role.
  • Dismissed: removed from the DOM; focus moves to the next logical element (the form's first field or the page heading).
  • Non-dismissible: anything that blocks buying, selling or getting paid (unverified, suspended, payout setup, offline) stays until resolved.

Responsive behaviour

  • Inline alerts take their container's width; actions wrap under the text below 480px.
  • Banner text and actions wrap and stay centred; the dismiss button stays top-right.
  • Banners stack above the top bar in the order danger, warning, info; never more than two at once (show the most severe and queue the rest).
  • The reservation banner and the checkout timer are the same message at two zoom levels: the banner for the page, the timer next to the pay button.

Theming

Four component tokens drive every tone: --alert-bg, --alert-fg, --alert-border and --alert-icon, defaulting to info and re-pointed by each tone modifier to --color-{tone}-bg|fg|border|icon. In the dark theme those status tokens become deep tints with light text, so the same markup works unchanged.

.alert--warning { --alert-bg: var(--color-warning-bg); --alert-fg: var(--color-warning-fg);
                  --alert-border: var(--color-warning-border); --alert-icon: var(--color-warning-icon); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A plain <div>. Add role="alert" only to danger alerts inserted after page load (a failed submit, a declined card) so they interrupt; success and info alerts inserted after load use role="status". Alerts rendered with the page (suspended account, refunded order) need no role: they are read in order. The error summary variant is focused and links to each field (see form layout).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches actions and the dismiss button in order.
Enter, SpaceActivates the action or dismisses.
EscNo effect; alerts are part of the page, not overlays.

Focus

The shared focus ring on actions and dismiss. A focused error summary shows the ring around the whole alert.

Labelling

Tone is never colour alone: each tone has its icon and the title says the problem. The icon is decorative (aria-hidden). Dismiss buttons are Dismiss. Links name their destination (Update bank details).

Motion

Alerts do not animate in; they appear where the content is. Dismissal removes them without a transition so layout does not jump twice.

Touch

Dismiss is a 24px target inset into the corner; small action buttons are 32px with 12px between them.

Content

  • Title states the situation in plain words: Your bank declined the card, not Payment error.
  • Text gives the reason and the next step, and what is safe: Try another card — the item is still reserved for you., Your listing is still live.
  • Errors from our side include the reference: Try again in a moment. Ref 5d1c-0a77.
  • Times are Eastern and specific: Planned maintenance Tue 27 Oct, 2:00–3:00 AM ET.
  • Never blame the member; never say Oops. See content and tone.
