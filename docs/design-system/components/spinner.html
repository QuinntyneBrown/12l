Spinner

A small turning ring for short waits of unknown length: checking an email link, loading an order confirmation, a button that is saving.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableStatus, not decoration

Overview

Use a spinner when nothing about the result's shape is known and the wait is short. When the layout is known (a listing grid, an order) use a skeleton; when the amount of work is known use a progress bar. Buttons show their own spinner through aria-busy; see button.

Anatomy

  1. Ring2px (3px large) --color-border-default ring with an accent arc; turns once per --duration-cycle-fast.
  2. Status textSays what is loading. The text, not the ring, is announced.

Checking your link…

A spinner has one state: turning. If the wait passes 10 seconds, replace it with a message and a way out (This is taking longer than usual. Try again).

Responsive behaviour

Spinners do not change with the viewport. Page-level spinners are centred in the content area, never over the top bar.

Theming

The ring is --color-border-default; the arc --color-accent (ink in light, paper in dark). Inside buttons the arc uses --btn-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The ring is aria-hidden="true". The adjacent text sits in role="status" so it is announced once; the busy region gets aria-busy="true".

Keyboard

Not focusable.

Focus

None. Focus stays where it was (on the button that started the wait).

Labelling

Always pair with visible text, or a visually hidden role="status" message (Loading your order).

Contrast

Arc on surface
Arc on canvas

Motion

One rotation per 800ms (--duration-cycle-fast). It keeps turning under reduced motion because it communicates status; the components stylesheet exempts it from the global reduced-motion rule.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • Status text uses a present participle and an ellipsis: Checking your link…, Loading your order…
  • Never Please wait on its own.
