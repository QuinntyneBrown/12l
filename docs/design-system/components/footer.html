Footer

The quiet end of every member page: the small logo, the policy links and the copyright.

Overview

The footer holds what members look for rarely but must always find: Marketplace rules, Privacy, Terms and Cookie settings (which opens the cookie dialog). It sits 96px below the content. Admin screens and auth screens have no footer; dialogs never do.

Anatomy

  1. LogoSmall (18px) logo linking home.
  2. Footer nav<nav aria-label="Footer">, links underline on hover.
  3. Copyright© 2026 12L.

Responsive behaviour

  • The three parts wrap onto separate lines below about 600px; links wrap with 8px row and 24px column gaps.
  • Padding follows --layout-margin.

Theming

Text --color-fg-muted on the canvas, a hairline --color-border-default top edge. Links inherit the muted colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <footer> (contentinfo) landmark containing a <nav aria-label="Footer">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabLogo, then each link.
EnterFollows the link; Cookie settings opens the dialog.

Focus

Shared focus ring.

Labelling

The footer nav is labelled Footer so it is distinct from Primary.

Contrast

Footer text

Motion

None.

Touch

Links are text with 8px / 24px gaps; on phones each wraps onto its own row for a larger target.

Content

  • Policy page names exactly as their headings.
  • Copyright as © 2026 12L; no “All rights reserved”.
