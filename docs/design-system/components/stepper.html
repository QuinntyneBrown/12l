Stepper

Shows progress through fixed stages. Orders use the timeline (Paid, Shipped, Delivered, Completed); the numbered stepper is the core form variant.

Overview

The order timeline sits at the top of order and sale detail. It is status, not navigation: the steps are not links, and the dates say when each happened. The current step is drawn in signal red, completed steps in ink, upcoming in the hairline colour.

The numbered stepper (.stepper) is for multi-step forms. 12L's sell and checkout flows are single pages with sections, so no mock uses it yet; keep it for a flow that genuinely cannot fit on one page.

Anatomy

  1. Completed segment3px ink bar; label default colour.
  2. Current segment3px --color-brand bar; aria-current="step".
  3. Label and dateBold step name over a tabular date; upcoming steps have no date.

Responsive behaviour

  • The timeline keeps four columns down to 320px; labels are short enough to fit.
  • Below 768px the numbered stepper hides every label except the current step's, keeping the numbers.

Theming

Upcoming bars --color-border-default, completed --color-fg-default, current --color-brand. Labels move from --color-fg-muted to default once reached.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An ordered list with aria-label="Order progress"; the current step has aria-current="step". Steps are not interactive.

Keyboard

Not focusable; nothing to operate. Actions for the current step (Add tracking, Confirm receipt) are buttons below the timeline.

Focus

None.

Labelling

Step names are text; completed state is conveyed by the date and by list position, and aria-current marks the current step so colour is not the only cue.

Content

  • Step names are the order statuses members see elsewhere: Paid, Shipped, Delivered, Completed.
  • Dates as Fri 23 Oct; no times.
